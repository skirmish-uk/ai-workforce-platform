# Business Context

The business is an Australian-focused AI workforce technology company.

The proposition is to help SMEs adopt advanced AI without needing to understand AI infrastructure.

The platform creates tailored AI employees that are configured around each customer's:
- business knowledge
- processes
- systems
- permissions
- workflows
- objectives

The business aims to improve:
- productivity
- operational efficiency
- response times
- automation
- digital capability
- competitiveness

Grant applications should frame the technology project around development and commercialisation of a scalable platform for secure, business-specific AI deployment, rather than simply requesting funding for a services agency.

Potential future verticals include:
- marketing agencies
- law firms
- insurance agencies
- manufacturers
- wholesalers
- real estate agencies

Initial implementation should avoid high-regulation verticals until the platform has mature governance.

The commercial model is recurring managed access to the AI workforce, potentially combined with an implementation/setup fee.
