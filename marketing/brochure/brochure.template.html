Common questions

What people usually ask us.

Will people know they're not talking to a person?

Yes. It introduces itself as an AI assistant at the start of every conversation. Most people just want a quick, correct answer, and they get one.

What happens if it doesn't know the answer?

It says it isn't sure, takes the person's details and what they need, and passes the message to you. It doesn't make things up.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. We set everything up. To change your hours or prices, send us an email or a photo of your new price list.

Can it sound like me or my receptionist?

Yes. With the person's written permission, we can record a short sample of their voice and use it for your assistant. Otherwise we choose a friendly Australian voice with you.

Can it answer my business phone number?

That's coming soon. Today it answers people on your website and by voice on a computer or tablet. Ask us about joining the trial for phone answering.
